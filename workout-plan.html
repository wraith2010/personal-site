The Point03 Levers

Chase the load

Bam Bam is the strength lever. Log every top set — you can't beat a number you didn't write down. Add reps or weight each cycle, not each day.

Two hard cardio days, no more

The Norwegian 4×4 is the highest-return cardio protocol there is. Twice a week is plenty — a third would just eat the recovery that Bam Bam needs.

Miles are the glue

Two rides, 12+ miles each, on whichever days the weather cooperates. Mostly conversational — this is aerobic volume, not a third interval session.
